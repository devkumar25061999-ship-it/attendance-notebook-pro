Privacy Policy for Attendance Notebook Pro

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Attendance Notebook Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application across app stores including Indus Appstore, Google Play, and Vivo V-Appstore.

1. Data Collection & Local Storage

Attendance Notebook Pro is designed with privacy-first principles:

  • Local Sandbox Storage: All your attendance logs, duty statuses (Work, Half-day, Holiday, Leave), overtime hours, daily wages, and diary notes are stored exclusively on your device using local browser/app storage.
  • No Cloud Sync Required: We do not upload, store, or process your personal attendance records on any external server. You have full control to backup or export your data as JSON files.

2. Camera & Face Punch Permissions

If you choose to use the optional "Face Punch" attendance feature:

  • Camera access is requested solely to capture your attendance selfie on-device.
  • No biometric facial data, templates, or camera streams are transmitted or stored remotely.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

To support the development and maintenance of this app, we integrate Google AdMob:

  • AdMob may use device identifiers and advertising IDs to serve relevant ads in compliance with Google Developer Policies.
  • You can manage ad personalization settings directly within your Android device settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is intended for professional attendance and wage tracking for working professionals and contractors.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our developer contact information provided on the Indus Appstore or Google Play Store listing.
